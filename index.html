ABOUT LATHAMIX SPECIFIED

Professional technique. Local roots. Original sound.

Hi, I'm Lateef Hammed, a music producer born and raised in Ibadan, Nigeria.

What started as a lifelong love for sound has grown into a career dedicated to creating music with character, energy, and identity. I studied music production at Royal Revolution Music College, where I developed the technical foundation behind my craft.

Today, I combine professional production techniques with the sounds and influences rooted in my environment to create compelling beats and tracks for artists and listeners.

When I'm not in the studio layering sounds and shaping ideas, you'll find me exploring the vibrant music culture around Ibadan and drawing inspiration from the sounds around me.
